# Ambit — install note for beta friends

_Phase 8.2 T6.1. Paste the block below into a text message as-is. No screenshots on purpose:
they date the moment a screen changes. Keep it under twenty lines._

---

Hi — I've built a small thing called Ambit and I'd love you to try it for a week.

It's a calm, endless feed of public-domain pictures and articles, loosely tuned to what you
pick. No likes, no comments, no followers, no ads. Just look at things.

1. Open **https://ambit.benreilly.io** on your phone.
2. Sign up with **the exact email address I invited** (same spelling), and any password.
3. Pick a few topics when it asks. You can change them later under Profile → Topics.
4. Put it on your home screen so it feels like an app:
   - **iPhone:** in Safari, tap Share → scroll down → **Add to Home Screen** → Add.
   - **Android / desktop Chrome:** tap **Add** on the "Keep Ambit close" card (or ⋮ → Install app).
5. Scroll. Tap a picture to see it full-screen; swipe sideways to wander. Save what you like —
   saving nudges what you see next.

It'll be quiet and a bit odd at first. That's the point.

One ask: **tell me the first thing that felt wrong** — a screen, a word, a picture, anything.
Reply here whenever it happens.
