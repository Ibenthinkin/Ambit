1a, everywhere
The pair is always the first row after the thing itself. On pictures that's directly under the image. On a spread each work gets its own pair. On articles it waits until the end of the text, so it never interrupts reading. Frames are shown scrolled to where the pair sits. [Open the phone prototype..]
